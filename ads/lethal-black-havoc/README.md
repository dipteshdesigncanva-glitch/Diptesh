# LETHAL BLACK HAVOC — 25s UGC-style ad (9:16)

A spec ad for the **LETHAL BLACK HAVOC** 75% wireless mechanical keyboard (₹6,449). It is built entirely from a screen recording of the brand's product page, plus a synthesized soundtrack and an AI creator voiceover.

**Deliverable:** `HAVOC_UGC_ad_9x16_25s.mp4`, 1080×1920 @ 30fps, H.264 12 Mbps, AAC 256k, exactly 25.0s.

## Structure

The soundtrack is a 144 BPM phonk/trap beat, and 60 beats make exactly 25s. Every cut, slam, pop and impact sits on that grid.

## Sound

`ugc_audio.py` generates everything:

- the beat: kick, distorted 808, clap, hats with rolls, a cowbell riff and an intro pad
- keyboard thocks, whooshes, risers, impacts, UI pops, counter ticks, star dings, a price "ching", a tap click and glitch hits

There are no third-party samples or music. The voiceover is Kokoro TTS (offline, voice `af_heart`), generated by `vo.py`. The music is ducked under the voice, and the mix is limited to about -13.5 dBFS RMS.

## Re-render

```bash
./extract_clips.sh path/to/recording.mp4       # frames + clips, with the mouse cursor painted out
python3 ugc_audio.py vo out_audio.f32           # soundtrack + VO mix
python3 ugc_ad.py video.mp4                     # ~2.5 min on 4 cores
ffmpeg -i video.mp4 -f f32le -ar 44100 -ac 2 -i out_audio.f32 -map 0:v -map 1:a \
  -c:v libx264 -b:v 12M -pix_fmt yuv420p -c:a aac -b:a 256k -shortest -movflags +faststart ad.mp4
```

Preview stills: `python3 ugc_ad.py stills/ 2,150,600` writes those frame numbers as JPGs.

`assets/` already holds the cutouts and mattes. To rebuild them, run `prep_ugc.py` (rembg isnet) and then `prep_ugc2.py` (luminance mattes with hole-fill). The voiceover lines live in `vo.py`, and their placements are `VO_AT` in `ugc_ad.py` and `ugc_audio.py`.

Requirements: Python 3 with `numpy` and `Pillow`, plus `ffmpeg`. Re-cutting assets also needs `rembg`, and regenerating the voiceover needs `kokoro-onnx`. The Inter and Noto Color Emoji system fonts are used. Anton is SIL OFL.
